# Kickoff briefs

One brief per research domain of `../PLAN.md` section 4. A brief is the whole context a research agent or a human researcher gets; it must be enough to produce the packet described in PLAN section 5.1 without reading the SDL.

Every brief has the same sections: scope and out of scope · what exists today (fragments, node types) · ecosystem to survey · seed questions · retrieval patterns to consider · sources and tools to score · known problems and open questions · deliverables. Seed questions are starting points, not the list; the packet replaces them.

Packet output goes to `../packets/<id>/`. Interview records go to `../interviews/`. Program decisions go to `../decisions/`.
