Notes from the model stage (A6, 2026-10-08; ids unchanged)

- Every row has a section in `03-retrieval-patterns.md`. The D7 schema answers them with 4 node types (Organization, Person, Brand, Award) and 7 relationship types (HOLDS_ROLE, HOLDS_STAKE, RECEIVES_VALUE_FROM, PARENT_OF, OWNS_BRAND, AWARDED_BY, AWARDED_TO); everything else is a kernel Assertion predicate, a kernel Assessment subkind, or a seam edge declared by another module.
- `ConflictRelevanceAssessment` is the kernel `Assessment` kind CONFLICT_RELEVANCE with D7-owned subkind values (CQ-CL-05, Q-D7-06); Q-D7-08 uses EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS `D7.role-agreement`, Q-D7-20 / Q-D7-24 use QUALITY `D7.disclosure-coverage`, Q-D7-23 uses INSIGHT `D7.undisclosed-tie-lead`.
- Q-D7-04 financings are D13 Events (amounts are Assertions on the Event); D7 contributes the related persons (HOLDS_ROLE) and named holders (HOLDS_STAKE).
- Q-D7-05 / Q-D7-15 rely on the new `Award` node; the study side needs D3 to declare Study → Award (OQ-D7-04).
- Q-D7-10 has no typed operator edge: a clinic operator doing business under a brand is an OWNS_BRAND episode with an Assertion qualifier (OQ-D7-22).
