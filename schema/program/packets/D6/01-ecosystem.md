## 0. What the domain is, in one paragraph

A consumer product reaches a person through a chain of artifacts, each made by a different actor and each able to disagree with the others: a **label** (what the maker declares per serving), a **trade-item identity** (GTIN/UPC, retailer product codes, ASINs, Shopify SKUs), a **listing** on a storefront or marketplace (what the retailer shows, often copied and sometimes stale), an **offer** (who sells, who ships, at what price kind), **price observations** over time (by the merchant itself, by trackers, by archives, by journalists), and **events** that change any of these (relabels, package changes, discontinuations, recalls, certification changes). The research below found real cases of every one of those disagreements for a single product family (Tru Niagen 300 mg), plus a prepared food from a named organization (McDonald's Big Mac), a packaged prepared food sold through a podcast sponsorship (David protein bar), and a Class I recall sold through Amazon (MOGO moringa capsules).

### 1.5 Regulators and public health

- **Label law.** 21 CFR 101.36 governs Supplement Facts. For proprietary blends: "The quantitative amount by weight specified for the proprietary blend shall be the total weight of all other dietary ingredients contained in the proprietary blend" [S39]. → per-ingredient amounts inside a blend are legally absent; AG1's DSLD records show blends ("Alkaline, Nutrient-Dense Raw Superfood Complex 7388 mg", "Nutrient Dense Extracts, Herbs & Antioxidants 2732 mg") whose members have no amounts [S7][S8].
- **FDA recalls and enforcement reports.** openFDA food enforcement records: F-1109-2024, Nordic Naturals, "Nordic Naturals Baby s Vitamin D3 liquid, 400 IU (10 mcg) D3, 0.76 fl oz (22.5 mL) DIETARY SUPPLEMENT", reason "Elevated levels of vitamin D3", Class II, 5564 bottles, "Lot number 234909", distribution text "Number of consignees who purchased through Amazon are unknown.", status Terminated [S27]. H-1128-2026, MOGO Moringa LLC, Class I, "Product may be contaminated with Salmonella Typhimurium", 23,186 bottles, lot codes 15525AA and 00926AA, "Products were sold nationwide via ecommerce (Amazon, Ebay, Etsy, and mogomiranga.com)", status Ongoing [S28]. A query for supplement recalls mentioning Amazon in the distribution pattern returned 4 records [S29].
- **Company recall announcement vs agency investigation (MOGO).** The company notice says "Mogo Moringa LLC does not authorize any third‑party sellers to distribute its products, and customers are encouraged to purchase only from official sources." and "Independent third‑party laboratory testing was conducted on retained samples from the affected lots, and no _Salmonella_ was detected." [S30]. FDA's investigation page says the product was "Sold at online stores including Amazon, eBay, and mogomoringa.com.", that state health departments in Washington, Tennessee and Florida collected open containers positive for the outbreak strain, that 34 people were infected, 11 hospitalized, and that "FDA has increased screening of imported moringa powder from India for _Salmonella_." [S31]. The same FDA page says "from 14 states" in its narrative and lists 22 state codes under "States with Cases" [S31]. → harm, authorized-channel and disconfirmation questions all meet in one case.
- **Processes.** Firms initiate recalls; FDA classifies (Class I/II), publishes an enforcement report and sometimes a press release; outbreak pages are updated in place ("Current Update", "Previous Updates") [S27][S28][S30][S31]. Lots, not GTINs, are the recall unit in these records [S27][S28].

- **Retail-purchase label-accuracy studies.** Of 30 weight-loss supplements bought online from companies advertising military discounts, "25 had inaccurate labels" (PMID 38691359, doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.9131) [S40]. Andrographolide content in 40 *A. paniculata* products ranged "from 29% to 174% of the labelled dose", three online-bought products contained toxic contaminants (PMID 41397381, doi:10.57187/s.4728) [S42]. Red yeast rice supplements from an EU e-commerce platform: about 11% raised safety concern and about 35% of one subset had non-compliant labeling (PMID 38928859, doi:10.3390/foods13121919) [S43]. Melatonin gummies quantity research letter (PMID 37097362, doi:10.1001/jama.2023.2296; no abstract, no numbers quoted) [S41]. → measured content vs declared content is a first-class harm question; the measurement lives in D8, the product identity and the purchase channel in D6.

## 3. Findings that shape the model (cross-lens)

### 3.1 Identity: one "30-count Tru Niagen 300 mg" has at least two GTINs and two retailer items, with no composition change on the labels

- DSLD labels V09.2 (entered 2023-05-24) and V12.3 (entered 2025-04-24) both carry UPC "8 50015 31111 6" and both declare Niagen 300 mg as Nicotinamide Riboside Chloride (UNII 8XM2XT8VWI) per capsule with the same three other ingredients [S1][S2]. Between them, directions changed from "Adults take one capsule daily" (max daily servings 1) to "Adults take one capsule 1-3 times daily as needed" (max daily servings 3), and the seals changed (NSF Certified Sport removed, Alkemist Assured added) [S1][S2]. DSLD relates 323170 to 295562 as "Image difference, same product" [S2].
- The brand store, Amazon and iHerb's current item use GTIN 850015311857 for the 30-count [S9][S13][S18]; the brand store already used 850015311857 on 2025-05-20 [S15]; iHerb marks its UPC 850015311116 item discontinued and calls the 850015311857 item a "New formulation" [S17][S18]. The 90-count label in DSLD carries UPC "8 56029 00855 4" [S3] while the brand store's 90-count barcode is 850015311895 [S13].
- → Round-0001's open question (variant vs formulation vs package) gets a concrete fixture: a label-version change (directions, seals) and a GTIN/package change, while composition is unchanged on every label we hold; the retailer's "new formulation" is a claim to be adjudicated, not a fact (inference).

### 3.2 Price is plural: kind, observer, seller role, and time

Same 30-count on 2026-10-08: brand store $49.00 (subscription with no platform-level discount) [S13]; Amazon one-time $49.00, S&S $44.10 / $41.65, per count $1.63 [S9]; iHerb $49.00, $1.63/serving [S18]; 180-count $244.00 at the brand store (a kit of two 90s) vs $238.00 on Amazon [S9][S13]. Over time: DTC 30-count at 4900 cents in captures of 2024-10-09 and 2025-11-30 [S16]; Amazon-sold lowest $42.99 on Oct 31, 2024, third-party lowest $30.74 on Jan 14, 2022 [S19]. → price observations need: price kind (one-time, subscription tier, per unit, bundle, list/compare-at), currency, observedAt, observer (merchant page, archive, tracker, journalist), seller-role context, and the offer they belong to; price per effective dose must be computed at query time from a price observation, a package count and a label amount (inference).

### 3.3 Harm, risk and disconfirmation are commerce-shaped

- Recalls name lots, channels in free text, and firms, not GTINs or listings [S27][S28]; a recalled product sold on Amazon/eBay/Etsy can stay in homes ("does have a long shelf life") [S31].
- The recalling firm's own statements can contradict the agency record (no authorized third-party sellers vs sold on Amazon/eBay; no Salmonella in retained samples vs outbreak strain in open containers) [S30][S31].
- Over-dosing is a product-level hazard (vitamin D3 at elevated levels in an infant product) [S27].
- Label opacity is legal (proprietary blends declare a total only) and blocks dose questions [S7][S39].
- Measured content often differs from declared content in products bought online [S40][S42][S43].
- Certification scope and label seals diverge over time [S1][S2][S20].

### 3.4 Prepared and packaged foods are products with formulations and organizations behind them

McDonald's publishes a per-component formulation with allergens and an as-of statement, but no GTIN and no price; a third party (The Economist) publishes the price series [S24][S25][S26]. David publishes UPC, macros and ingredients, but its own page states soy two ways (flavor line: soy only as "May Contain"; FAQ: soy lecithin and "Contains … soy"), and a crowd database holds different nutrition for the same GTIN (140 vs 150 kcal) [S35][S36]. → a prepared food is a D6 Product (sold, priced, changes over time) whose composition semantics (FoodItem, preparation, nutrients) belong to D12; the menu item's maker/brand/seller is a D7 Organization (inference consistent with the coordinator's ownership map).

## 4. The four lenses

- **Science.** Products are the bridge between evidence and use: the label (declared form and amount, e.g. NRC as the chloride salt [S1]) is what dose and applicability reasoning starts from; measured content diverges (label-accuracy studies [S40][S42][S43]); certification and CoA claims are attached at different scopes [S20]. The product layer must keep declared vs measured vs claimed apart and keep the label version that was on sale at a date.
- **Economics.** Channel structure (DTC, marketplace with FBA, specialty retail, practitioners) [S23]; price kinds and subscriptions [S9][S13]; kits and bundles [S12][S13]; reseller/partnership catalogs [S33]; affiliate networks and sponsorships [S14][S37]; segment sales [S23]; prepared-food price series by a third party [S25]. Investors ask about channels and price trends; biohackers about cost per effective dose.
- **Community.** Podcast sponsorship pages and vanity URLs [S37]; testimonials on product pages [S35]; crowd-sourced food records from logging apps [S36]; review counts on marketplaces [S9][S17]. These are D9 artifacts whose targets are D6 products and offers.
- **AI (agent-readiness).** Agent-ready: DSLD, Shopify JSON, Wayback CDX, openFDA, EDGAR, Economist CSV, Open Food Facts by barcode [S1][S12][S14][S22][S25][S27][S36]. Scrape-only: Amazon, iHerb, NSF listings, restaurant pages, CamelCamelCamel, Amazon policy pages [S9][S17][S19][S20][S24][S38]. Blocked today: FDC (no key), Keepa/PA-API/GS1/impact.com (accounts). Recurring gaps: identifiers as free text (lots, channels), images instead of text (Nutrition Facts panels), self-contradicting regulator pages, malformed tracker identifiers [S19][S28][S31][S35].
