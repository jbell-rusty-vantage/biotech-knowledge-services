## Evidence for

In adults reporting poor sleep, 250 mg/day of elemental magnesium given as magnesium bisglycinate for four weeks reduced Insomnia Severity Index scores more than placebo (-3.9 vs -2.3 points; p = 0.049) [a-dose-mg] [a-isi-between]. The between-group effect size was small (Cohen's d = 0.2) [a-effect-size]. The same capsules delivered 1523 mg/day of glycine [a-dose-glycine].

## Open questions

Sleep outcomes in this trial were self-reported only, with no objective measure [a-limitation]. The design does not separate a magnesium effect from a glycine effect.
