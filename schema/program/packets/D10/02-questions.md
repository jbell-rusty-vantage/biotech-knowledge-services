# D10 — high-value questions

Stage: research (Wave 1), 2026-10-08. Rows that already exist in `biotech-meta/docs/schema/ontology-lab/competency-questions.md` keep their `CQ-*` id and say "(existing CQ, reused)"; new rows are `Q-D10-nn`. Answer kinds follow PLAN 6.1: **retrieved** (computed at query time over asserted edges, assessments and criteria; nothing stored), **materialized** (regenerable projection with a method version and input digest), **assessment** (immutable, versioned, supersedable judgment node with inputs and producer), **published** (insert-only skeleton in the graph citing assertions, assessments, locators and a PolicyVersion; body in the store). A comparison or ranking is *retrieved* until BellLabs publishes it, then it is *published* (directive 3.5). Value: 1 = the product cannot ship without it; 2 = foundational; 3 = expansion.

Polarity uses the contract's three values. Classification rule (adversarial-review stage, 2026-10-08): a row is `harm` only when its answer is about harm, risk, avoidance or the disconfirmation of a benefit claim for an option or entity; audit and staleness queues (CQ-AX-08, CQ-AX-11, Q-D10-13, CQ-RC-07), secondary-source misstatements (Q-D10-18) and disagreements between rankings (Q-D10-11) are `neutral` even when their findings can feed a harm answer. Negative-side rows are: CQ-RC-06, Q-D10-03, Q-D10-04, Q-D10-06, Q-D10-07, Q-D10-08, Q-D10-09, Q-D10-10, Q-D10-16, Q-D10-21, Q-D10-23, Q-D10-24, Q-D10-25 — 13 of 36 rows (36%). Disconfirmation rows are marked `harm` in the polarity column because they ask for the evidence against a benefit claim.
