## The model in one paragraph

Six node types: **Target** (the goal vocabulary), **Intervention** (practices, procedures and named regimens; one node with a `kind`), **Protocol → ProtocolEdition → ProtocolStep** (identity, immutable content, ordered steps with dose ranges, timing, stated purpose and review rules), and **UseConstraint** (the shared blocking identity many sources resolve to). Six relationship types: HAS_EDITION (the only episode edge), HAS_STEP, STEP_USES (target: the kernel interface `Subjectable`, admissible types executor-validated), STATES_CONSTRAINT (derived), RESOLVES_TO_CONSTRAINT (derived) and OPERATIONALIZED_BY (union 3). Everything else D5 knows — what helps or hurts a target, contraindications, interactions, adverse effects, upper limits, authorship — is a kernel `Assertion.predicate` with a mandatory population qualifier, and every weighed conclusion (safety signal, applicability, step evidence, target-effect synthesis) is a kernel `Assessment`. No D5 property or edge says good, bad, safe or helps.

## Decisions (alternatives considered, what was cut and why)

**D-01 Treatment, Procedure, Lifestyle and the practice side of Exposure collapse into one `Intervention` with a closed `kind`.** Alternatives: (a) keep Treatment and Procedure (pinned W06) plus Lifestyle (W05) = 3 types; (b) one Intervention with kind (chosen); (c) no Intervention at all, every means is a D1/D6/D12 record. Evidence: protocols interleave every kind in one ordered list (Huberman: light, temperature, caffeine timing, an app practice, supplements; Blueprint: exercise, meal timing, laser, prescription drugs, supplements, measurement; AgelessRx: drug program with labs), and no question traverses Treatment separately from Procedure or Lifestyle; the kind only changes which source classes exist (Q-D5-28). (c) fails Q-D5-25 (trial arm administering "endurance training" or "intermittent low-dose rapamycin") and Q-D5-17 (clinic offerings) and leaves practices (sauna, sunlight) without a subject for target and constraint assertions. Rule kept from research: **plain supplement intake does not mint an Intervention**; the step points at the D1 / D6 record with its dose. Cut with it: TreatmentModality, TreatmentComponentRole, TreatmentIntentKind/Basis enums (no question filters them), USES_COMPONENT (predicate INTERVENTION_INCLUDES, named HAS_COMPONENT until the adversarial review).

**D-02 Target is the vocabulary node; the pinned numeric "Target" leaves the name.** The pinned W16 Target (a source-stated threshold for one metric, "plasma lithium 0.6–0.8 mmol/L") becomes `thresholdComparator/Low/High/Unit` on a MEASURE or REVIEW step whose STEP_USES points at the D4 Metric. FunctionalGoal is renamed Target (owner's word, directive 3.1). Hierarchy is a property (`parentKey`), not an edge: two levels, 15 nodes, and every query expands it with `t.key = $k OR t.parentKey = $k`.

**D-03 The step→Target conflict is settled with a property, not an edge and not an assertion per step.** Options: (a) structural STEP_FOR_TARGET edge (1 type, 1 pair; contradicts the vocabulary seam rule); (b) an INTENDED_FOR_TARGET assertion per step (no type, but one extra Assertion + locator + adjudication per step for something the edition already captures); (c) `ProtocolStep.targetKeys: [String!]` (chosen). A step is not a means but captured, located, hashed content of an edition; its stated purpose is part of that content and inherits the edition's provenance (attach Assertion, step locator). The property is filterable, hashed into the step payload, costs nothing, and matches D13's `Frontier.targetKeys` device. The seam rule is amended in `vocab-targets.yaml` 0.2.0 (exception limited to ProtocolStep; `targetKeys` on any means stays forbidden). Protocol-level purpose ("Toolkit for Sleep") is one INTENDED_FOR_TARGET assertion on the Protocol.

**D-04 ProtocolStep absorbs MeasurementPlan, ProtocolAdjustmentRule, the numeric Target and the pinned StepDoseProperties edge.** One step = one action with one primary means; "A or B" becomes sibling steps sharing `alternativeGroup` (Huberman's 145 mg threonate OR 200 mg bisglycinate). Doses are node properties (`doseMin/doseMax/doseUnit/dosePeriod/intakeScope`) so Q-D5-06 can filter and compare them in GraphQL and Cypher; the alternative (dose on the STEP_USES edge) needs a custom edge-property type outside the kernel's four and is harder to filter. Pinned edition-level cadence fields (20+) moved to steps; the edition keeps only payload identity and observation bounds. DEPENDS_ON became `dependsOnStepKeys` (CQ-PR-02 reads dependencies inside one edition; nothing traverses them across editions).

**D-05 STEP_USES targets the kernel interface `Subjectable` (adversarial review; was a typed 6-member union StepMeans).** The check stage kept the union for typing although it measured +2.9 KB / +8 inputs over `Subjectable`. The review showed the cost was not only bytes: the union sat at the directive's limit of 6, could not admit D12 `DietaryPattern` (or D4 `Device`), and so D5 kept its own InterventionKind DIETARY_PATTERN while D12 kept DietaryPattern and D3's AdministeredMaterial admitted both — one diet could be minted twice. Slimmer and with one home per diet: STEP_USES → `Subjectable`, InterventionKind DIETARY_PATTERN retired (time-restricted eating is a LIFESTYLE_PRACTICE; diets as composition are D12 DietaryPattern). What the union enforced moves to the executor: admissible types are D1 ChemicalSubstance | IngredientMaterial, D6 Product, D12 FoodItem | FoodCategory | DietaryPattern, D5 Intervention, D4 Metric | Device, validated per stepKind (MEASURE → Metric; TAKE → substance, material, product, food); 10-seams lists each admissible type so owners keep them Subjectable. GraphQL clients read `uses { __typename ... on ChemicalSubstance { … } }` as before. Pinned USES (union 6) and EMPLOYS (union 6) merged into this one field (instruments are D6 Products; measured quantities are D4 Metrics). Cost: −2.9 KB / −8 inputs measured; the map's expanded pairs rise (interface over every implementer), which A1 measured as ≈ 0 executable cost.

**D-06 UseConstraint stays as the shared blocking identity; contraindication, interaction, adverse effect and upper limit are Assertion predicates, not specialization types.** Pinned ContraindicationAssertion and InteractionAssertion re-declared subject/object/evidence on specializations (the bloat PLAN section 1 names). They become kernel Assertions with predicates USE_CONSTRAINED_IN, INTERACTS_WITH, HAS_UPPER_LIMIT, HAS_AUTHORIZED_DOSE_RANGE, CAUSES_ADVERSE_EFFECT (05 `predicateOnly`). The level each source states (label "must not be taken", clinic "interaction", forum deliberate use) is per asserter and lives in `Assertion.valueString` as a ConstraintLevel code with the verbatim directive in `proposition`; the level is never part of the constraint identity and never a property of the constraint (that would be a "how bad" property, directive 3.2). ConstraintLevel gains ADJUST_DOSE (sirolimus label, cannabidiol), SEPARATE_ADMINISTRATION (bisphosphonates "at least 2 hours"), DISCONTINUE_BEFORE (AgelessRx "one month before surgery").

**D-07 UseConstraint's subject and scope are not typed edges (CONSTRAINS_USE_OF and CONSTRAINT_SCOPE cut).** Pinned: CONSTRAINS_USE_OF → union 8, CONSTRAINT_SCOPE → union 10 (both over the limit). Options: (a) keep both, trimmed to unions of 5 and 6 (+2 types, +11 pairs); (b) keep only CONSTRAINS_USE_OF (+1 type, +5 pairs); (c) cut both (chosen). Every UseConstraint exists only because at least one Assertion resolved to it, and that Assertion already carries the subject (HAS_SUBJECT) and the scope entity (HAS_OBJECT: Condition or co-exposure) through the kernel, with population codes in qualifiers; the constraint's own `scopePopulations`, dose band, route and jurisdiction are filterable properties, and `identityKey` is the canonical composite key. All eight questions that need the subject or scope (Q-D5-03/04/05/11/21/23, CQ-RC-06, Q-D5-19) are written in 03 through `RESOLVES_TO_CONSTRAINT` + `HAS_SUBJECT`/`HAS_OBJECT` (two hops instead of one; GraphQL form `useConstraints(where: {resolvedFrom: {some: {subject: {some: {uid: {eq: $m}}}}}})`). Cost of (c): one extra hop on agent pre-checks (Q-D5-23), mitigated by the kernel's Assertion indexes; promote with (b) if fixture timing shows it matters (11-open-questions OQ-D5-13).

**D-08 RESOLVES_TO_CONSTRAINT is derived.** Resolution is a deterministic identity rule (`use-constraint-key/v2` since the adversarial review: kind, subject uid, object uid, population codes, dose band, route, jurisdiction and timing window → identityKey; v1 had no timing component, so two TIMING constraints on one means collided, OQ-D5-29), regenerable, read-only, with `DerivedEdgeProperties` (no confidence, no level). `UseConstraint.timingWindow` is the typed timing component; `exposureChange` was dropped from the node (a curated, unsourced direction on a projection-minted identity; asserters may disagree, so it stays an Assertion qualifier). Declared on UseConstraint with `direction: IN` because Assertion is a kernel type (kernel pattern rule 10). Alternative (asserted, one Assertion per resolution) would need an assertion about an assertion and adds nothing a question reads.

**D-09 STATES_CONSTRAINT replaces pinned HAS_CONSTRAINT → Constraint, and is derived (adversarial review).** Q-D5-11 (what the protocol states vs what it omits) is an anti-join on this edge; without it, stated cautions could only be found by comparing assertion locators with the protocol snapshot at query time — possible, but fragile. The check stage declared it structural, part of the immutable edition payload, while its target is minted by a regenerable projection: the v1 → v2 rule bump in this very review would have orphaned the edges or forced new edition payloads. Now the projection that mints the UseConstraint also emits STATES_CONSTRAINT, from the protocol author's caution Assertion (USE_CONSTRAINED_IN | INTERACTS_WITH | HAS_UPPER_LIMIT) whose locator is the step's own `locatorUid` (or lies inside its span on the same snapshot); `DerivedEdgeProperties`, `nestedOperations: []`, read-only. The step payload hashes the verbatim caution fields (statedLevel, conditionText), never constraint uids, so a rule bump re-projects edges and leaves editions untouched (all fixtures regenerated under v2 with unchanged edition hashes where the steps did not change). Alternative considered: drop the edge and compute "stated" as "a RESOLVES_TO_CONSTRAINT path from an Assertion by the protocol author citing a step locator" at query time (−1 type) — kept the edge because Q-D5-11 is value 1 and the anti-join is then one hop. Eligibility-type conditions ("if you are over 40") are not use constraints: they are `requirementLevel: CONDITIONAL` + `conditionText` + `appliesToPopulations` on the step. ConstraintRole edge properties were dropped (role derivable from `stepKind` + `statedLevel`).

**D-10 AdverseEffect is not a D5 node; effect terms are D2 Conditions.** Nausea, GI upset, hypermagnesemia, lymphoma, infection susceptibility are phenotypes or conditions; D2's research keeps `Condition` with a `conditionKind` (DISEASE, PHENOTYPE, RISK_STATE). CAUSES_ADVERSE_EFFECT points at a Condition; dose, schedule, route, severity (scheme + grade), frequency, context (therapeutic, overdose, accidental ingestion) and source kind are qualifiers. Contested: if D2 refuses, D5 re-mints AdverseEffect as node 7 with MedDRA/MeSH/HPO identifiers (budget allows it; OQ-D5-03).

**D-11 SafetySignal, applicability, step evidence, surveillance trend and target-effect synthesis are kernel Assessments.** Kinds SAFETY_SIGNAL, APPLICABILITY, EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS exist in the kernel enum; D5's subkinds and their `conclusionCode` / `gradeScheme` vocabularies are registered in the section "Registered assessment vocabulary" below (kernel pattern item 8, kernel decision M9: signal status → `conclusionCode`, severity → `gradeScheme` + `grade`). Inputs through kernel DERIVED_FROM; harm = `effectDirection: HARM`. A verdict on a **claim** is never a D5 assessment: the fixtures stage's `D5.claim-disconfirmation` subkind (an EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS carrying a claim verdict) was dropped in the review for the kernel claim lifecycle (VETTING Assessment → EVIDENCE_MISSION Activity → EVIDENCE_REPORT Report with the lattice verdict, directive 3.4), which Q-D5-14 now reads.

**D-12 HAS_EDITION is the only episode edge.** An edition is immutable; which edition was current when is the question (Q-D5-10 as-of, CQ-PR-01 intervals, Q-D5-13 overlap with D7 interest intervals, Q-D5-19 current editions). Pages are versioned in place and their displayed date lies (Huberman: "September 20, 2021" unchanged while the sponsor changed), so valid time comes from dated captures, and the edition records `firstObservedAt` / `lastObservedAt` with unobserved gaps left unknown. `StateEpisodeProperties` (kernel), EXCLUSIVE per protocol. An edition is minted only when the canonical step payload hash changes; a disclosure or sponsor change is a D7 interest assertion with valid time, not an edition (OQ-D5-08).

**D-13 OPERATIONALIZED_BY retargeted from D2 Outcome to D4 Biomarker | Metric.** D2's research proposes no Outcome node (goals → D5 Target, trial endpoints → D3 OutcomeDefinition, measurable quantities → D4); D4 proposes Biomarker and Metric and expects `Target → Metric` from D5. Study evidence reaches a target via D3 OutcomeDefinition → D4 Metric/Biomarker ← OPERATIONALIZED_BY. Curated (tool `ks-curation`), structural, recorded-only. **Check-stage amendment:** D2's SDL does keep a small curated `Outcome` node (the endpoint concept D3 `OutcomeDefinition -MEASURES->` points at: all-cause mortality, lifespan, handgrip strength), so the premise above was stale; D2 asked for it (D2 11-open-questions item 27) and the check stage restored `D2.Outcome` as a third TargetMeasure member (+1 pair, +643 B, +1 input type measured). Without it, target:longevity could not reach mortality endpoints that have no D4 metric. D3 path (D3 SDL): `StudyResult -RESULT_FOR-> OutcomeDefinition -MEASURES-> Metric | Biomarker | Outcome | Condition`. **Review-stage amendment:** the antioxidant fixture now has a curated Target → D2.Outcome edge (target:endurance → endurance running performance) and Q-D5-01's study branch is a real UNION branch; D2's and D3's seam files do not list the member yet (OQ-D5-43). The main traversers of this edge are D3 Q-D3-04 and D4 Q-D4-17 (05 `crossPacketQuestions`).

**D-14 Population is an enum plus a qualifier, not a node.** Constraints and target statements need non-study populations (pregnant, lactating, under 18, pre-surgery, immunosuppressed); D3 owns study populations. `PopulationGroup` is used typed on `UseConstraint.scopePopulations` and `ProtocolStep.appliesToPopulations` and as the value domain of the mandatory `population:` qualifier on every D5 predicate (`population:UNSPECIFIED` explicit). Diseases present are D2 Conditions, not codes. The kernel has no QUALIFIED_BY edge, so `vocab-targets.yaml` 0.1.0's qualifier assertions were withdrawn in 0.2.0.

**D-15 ProtocolEdition implements Subjectable** although the kernel says versioned states implement Entity only: applicability assessments (CQ-EV-04) and stated-purpose assertions are about an edition, and an implementer costs ≈ 0. Recorded as OQ-D5-15 for A1.

**D-16 A step's own locator is a property (`locatorUid` + `exactText`), not an edge.** The kernel forbids re-declaring `evidence` on domain types; the edition's attach Assertion cites every step locator through HAS_EVIDENCE, and the step names its own. Alternative: one Assertion per step (heavy) or a D5 edge to SourceLocator (a second evidence mechanism). OQ-D5-16 asks A1 whether a sanctioned pattern exists.

**D-17 UseConstraint stays; it is not the kernel `Claim` (adversarial review, model discipline).** The reviewers asked whether UseConstraint (a shared identity many asserters' statements resolve to) is a domain copy of the kernel Claim and RESOLVES_TO_CONSTRAINT a copy of OCCURRENCE_OF. Alternatives: (a) adopt Claim + OCCURRENCE_OF, with dose band and scope as Claim properties (−1 node type, −1 relationship type); (b) keep UseConstraint (chosen). What (a) cannot do without changing the kernel: (1) a Claim's identity is `propositionHash` over the normalized proposition **plus its binding (subject, predicate, object or literal, qualifiers)**, so the label's CONTRAINDICATED, a clinic's CONSULT_CLINICIAN and a forum's DELIBERATE_USE statements of one interaction are three Claims, and HAS_UPPER_LIMIT 350 mg vs a USE_CONSTRAINED_IN for the same population are different predicates — UseConstraint groups across levels and across the three predicates by construction (Q-D5-05, Q-D5-27 need exactly that grouping); (2) Q-D5-06 and Q-D5-23 filter typed `doseValue/doseUnit/dosePeriod/intakeScope`, `scopePopulations`, `route`, `timingWindow`, `jurisdiction`; putting them on Claim would add nine domain fields to a kernel type the kernel already warns could become a hub; (3) OCCURRENCE_OF is for `assertionKind: CLAIM` occurrences, and every label contraindication would enter the claim lifecycle (vetting queue, Q-K-19 "claims with no vetting"), which is the wrong workflow for regulator text. Where they meet: a community statement that is itself a popular claim ("grapefruit boosts rapamycin safely") is both a claim occurrence (OCCURRENCE_OF → Claim, vetted and attacked) and a safety Assertion that resolves to the UseConstraint; nothing is duplicated because the two links answer different questions. Recorded for A1 as OQ-D5-38 (kernel ownership).

**D-18 Protocol stays a node; it is not the kernel `Source` (adversarial review).** Alternative: ProtocolEdition -[IN]-> Source, with the protocol identity = the Source's `canonicalUri` (−1 node type). Rejected: a Source is one retrieval endpoint, a Protocol is a creator's enduring work. The Huberman toolkit moved from `hubermanlab.com/toolkit-for-sleep/` (2021 capture) to `www.hubermanlab.com/newsletter/toolkit-for-sleep` (2026) — two Sources, one protocol whose HAS_EDITION episodes must be one EXCLUSIVE chain (CQ-PR-01, Q-D5-10 as-of); book and clinic-handout protocols have no URL at all; and a Protocol is the subject of PROTOCOL_AUTHORED_BY and of interests over edition intervals (Q-D5-13), which a Source per URL would split. Cost: 1 node type, 1 field (`canonicalUri`, equal to the current Source's).

**D-19 Target is not D2's Outcome vocabulary (adversarial review).** D2's curated `Outcome` (lifespan, all-cause mortality, handgrip strength, VO2max, sleep quality) overlaps Target words. Disambiguation (now in the Target docstring and vocab-targets 0.2.2): a Target is what a person pursues (sleep, longevity), the object of the five goal predicates and of target-effect assessments; an Outcome is a study-independent endpoint concept that D3 OutcomeDefinitions MEASURE. The bridge is OPERATIONALIZED_BY (Target → Outcome | Metric | Biomarker), curated by D5. Merging them would make every trial endpoint a "goal" or every goal a scored endpoint; keeping two small curated vocabularies with one typed bridge costs one union member. Other modules' string keys into the vocabulary (D13 `Frontier.targetKeys`, D10 `DecisionCriterion.targetKeys`) are now listed in vocab-targets 0.2.2's exception (OQ-D5-47).

## Budget excess

No element is over budget (6 / 8 node types, 6 / 14 relationship types, 8 / 35 pairs, widest union 3 / 6, max 2 relationship fields per type), so there are no EXCESS lines.

## Risks

- **Two-hop constraint lookups.** Cutting CONSTRAINS_USE_OF / CONSTRAINT_SCOPE (D-07) makes the agent pre-check (Q-D5-23) traverse Assertion → subject; with many assertions per popular means (sirolimus, magnesium) the query depends on the kernel's `:Assertion(predicate)` index and on `RESOLVES_TO_CONSTRAINT` being kept current. Mitigation: fixture timing in the check stage; promotion path (b) in D-07.
- **Contested seams decide whether 6 stays 6.** AdverseEffect (D2), DietaryPattern (D12), the D3 arm → Intervention edge and the D6 offering edge are other modules' calls; if D2 refuses Condition for effect terms, D5 grows to 7 types and CAUSES_ADVERSE_EFFECT objects change.
- **String qualifiers carry the mandatory population.** `Assertion.qualifiers` is a list of strings; a missing `population:` entry is an executor validation, not a schema constraint. A typed kernel field would be safer (OQ-D5-05).
- **Level in `valueString`.** The ConstraintLevel code in a generic string field is validated by the executor, not the GraphQL type system (OQ-D5-14).
- **Executor-enforced means types.** With STEP_USES → `Subjectable` the GraphQL type no longer stops a step pointing at, say, a Study; the executor must validate the admissible types per stepKind (list in the SDL docstring and 05). A wrong target is a planner rejection, not a schema error.
- **Seam names can still drift.** The check stage verified type names but not edge names (HAS_INTEREST_IN, ASSOCIATION were wrong); the review re-read the relationship types of every 10-seams row in the sibling SDLs. Sibling files that still disagree with D5: D2 / D3 do not list OPERATIONALIZED_BY → Outcome (OQ-D5-43); D1 / D12 / D4 notes still say "union StepMeans" (OQ-D5-48); D10 uses RECOMMENDS_AGAINST with a Target object (OQ-D5-40); D9 proposes REPORTS_ADVERSE_EXPERIENCE (OQ-D5-41).
- **stepKey alignment** across in-place editions (OPEN-QUESTIONS P2-4) is a planner rule, not a schema guarantee; a wrong alignment shows up as spurious added/removed steps in CQ-PR-01.

## With twice the budget

Typed `CONSTRAINS_USE_OF` (UseConstraint → StepMeans-like union 5) and `CONSTRAINT_SCOPE` (→ Condition | co-exposure means, union 6) for one-hop constraint traversal; an `AdverseEffect` node with MedDRA PT/SOC hierarchy for pharmacovigilance-grade grouping; a `Population` node shared with D3 for non-study populations with definitions and identifiers; a typed asserted `AUTHORED_BY` (Protocol → Person | Organization) for Q-D5-13 at volume; a derived read-only `SERVES_TARGET` projection (means → Target, method-versioned) for low-latency "what is for T" lists; a `ProtocolSection` node for long multi-domain protocols (Blueprint) whose sections carry their own population and target.
