Evidence report (K fixture draft, not published): does resveratrol directly activate SIRT1 when the substrate carries a hydrophobic motif?
Hubbard et al. 2013 reported that specific hydrophobic motifs in SIRT1 substrates such as PGC-1α and FOXO3a facilitate SIRT1 activation by sirtuin-activating compounds, a class that includes resveratrol. [a-occ-hubbard-refined]
The same paper reported that activation of SIRT1 by resveratrol was attenuated when Glu230 was replaced by lysine. [a-hub-occ-e230k]
Pacholec et al. 2010 saw activation only with a peptide carrying a covalently attached fluorophore, which the 2013 authors tested as a bulky hydrophobic group that might substitute for hydrophobic amino acids of natural substrates. [a-occ-pacholec, adj-sup-refined]
Every supporting span comes from one investigator network and none comes from human studies, so the verdict is SUPPORTED_WITH_QUALIFICATION. [adj-sup-refined]
