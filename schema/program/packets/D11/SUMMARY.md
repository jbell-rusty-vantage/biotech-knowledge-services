# D11 — summary: bio-medical tooling action space

Check stage, A8, 2026-10-08; adversarial review and cross-packet pass (merged action space) applied the same day. Research only, not medical advice.

## What the domain is

D11 is the layer under every other domain. It decides whether an answer can be reached, identified, located and trusted. It covers which tools and sources can answer a question, how identifiers bridge across authorities, which spans our tools can re-select, and what failed when an answer is empty. Most of it is versioned config, not graph:
- `tooling/action-space.yaml`: the cross-packet registry, 167 merged entries (144 external, 23 internal) from all fourteen packets' `08` tables and tool logs, 402 packet-local aliases, 25 gaps ranked by blocked questions; scorecard in `tooling/readiness-scorecard.md`. D11's own 59 scored rows live in `_notes/action-space/d11-local-registry.yaml`.
- `identifier-bridges.yaml`: 33 schemes, 22 bridges, 7 recorded non-bridges, codes aligned to the K register v2.

The graph gets one record type, `CaptureAttempt`. It records a search, fetch, resolve or parse that FAILED or yielded only a PARTIAL capture (the non-captures the kernel cannot hold), generated by the run's Activity through kernel WAS_GENERATED_BY. An authoritative empty result is a kernel snapshot plus a FIELD_NOT_REPORTED Assertion, not an attempt.

## Five highest-value questions

1. **CQ-AX-04** (owner K). When the graph returns nothing, is that unknown, not reported by the source, measured absent, or out of scope? D11 evaluates K's states per tool; only `CaptureAttempt` can tell "blocked" from "never queried".
2. **Q-D11-05.** Which assertions and published answers rest on a source that has since been retracted or corrected, and since when do we know?
3. **Q-D11-03.** What material, form and dose did a study administer, quoted with its locator? This is the 3.8 path: chunk → assertion → locator → bytes.
4. **Q-D11-11.** Does a drug used off-label for longevity carry a boxed warning, in which label version, effective from when?
5. **Q-D11-17.** Which sources failed, were rate-limited or were blocked during mission M, and which questions did that leave unanswerable?

## Three biggest cuts

1. **ToolCapability / SourceAccessProfile node.** The registry stays config. The graph refers to a tool only by id: the `SourceSnapshot.captureMethod` prefix and `CaptureAttempt.toolId`. A ~60-member vocabulary node would be a fan-in hub that no question traverses past.
2. **IdentifierScheme and IdentifierBridge nodes, and SAME_AS-style identifier→identifier edges.** A bridge is the co-attachment of authority-asserted HAS_IDENTIFIER episodes on one entity, or a RESOLUTION / EQUIVALENCE Assessment.
3. **Every typed relationship (0 / 14).** Pinned SourceDiscoveryRecord's two edges and ATTEMPTED_FOR / YIELDED_SNAPSHOT became uid properties. Text-mined annotations stay derived MENTIONS. Five statements stay D11 `Assertion.predicate` values (SCREAMING_SNAKE); four that duplicated D5, D6 and D8 concepts were dropped in the adversarial review.

## Three biggest risks

1. **Tool renditions as evidence.** Some sources can only be captured as Firecrawl or Tavily renditions: the APS retraction notice is behind Cloudflare, and the NDI letter rendition lost a β. They are not issuer bytes, and their hashes vary while their quotes re-select. The kernel has no `TOOL_RENDITION` hash basis yet (open questions 3 and 25).
2. **Credential leakage and attempt noise.** Request URIs carry keys, and an adapter that records successes would multiply nodes. The executor must strip credential parameters before hashing and storing. It must also write attempts only for non-captures. Both belong to the G10 recorder, which does not exist yet, and its canonical `requestDigest` form is still open (open questions 28 and 33).
3. **Registry history is unobservable.** CT.gov record history is blocked for every client (403, plus a challenge page through Firecrawl). Questions about outcome switching or registration changes (Q-D11-07, D3:CQ-ST-08) can only use versions we observe ourselves from our first capture onward (gap G1, P0).
