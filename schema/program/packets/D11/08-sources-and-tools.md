## What broke

1. **Record history of ClinicalTrials.gov** (`ctgov-record-history`, readiness 0): `/api/int/studies/{nct}/history` → 403 for curl with any User-Agent and for `/history/1`; the history tab → bot challenge for Firecrawl. Registry diffs can only start at our first snapshot (gap G1).
2. **Shared-IP keys and quotas** (G6): USDA FDC `DEMO_KEY` 429 (`Retry-After: 10184`, limit 10, shared by every agent on the machine), Semantic Scholar unkeyed 429, OpenAlex unkeyed daily USD budget.
3. **Key or account walls**: CAS Common Chemistry 401; fhir.loinc.org redirects to login; regulations.gov attachments 403 to curl (Firecrawl gets them).
4. **Bot challenges**: journals.physiology.org (Cloudflare, 403 "Just a moment...") — publisher pages are rendition-only (`publisher-pages`, entry added in the fixtures stage).
5. **Timeouts**: WHO ICTRP timed out after 60 s for NCT04488601 while it had served NCT02921659 ten minutes earlier.
6. **Empty results that are not absences**: PubMed `NCT02921659[si]` count 0 although the paper names the trial in its full text (bridge B03 exists only inside JATS).
7. **Lossy renditions**: openFDA flattens SPL sections; Firecrawl PDF text drops "β"; Firecrawl HTML renditions carry random anchor ids (hash unstable); MCP connectors drop integrity fields.
8. **Text-mining mislinks**: Europe PMC annotations tag "nicotinamide riboside" as ChEBI dihydrozeatin riboside and "nicotinamide" as RxNorm Niacinamide; postfix overlaps exact.
9. **Client failures**: tvly console-encoding crash; curl cannot write MSYS paths (`/c/...`, exit 23); `python -I` ignores `PYTHONIOENCODING`.
