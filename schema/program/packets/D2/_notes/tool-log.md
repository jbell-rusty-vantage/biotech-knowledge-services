## Normalization finding (affects SourceLocator `normalizationVersion`)

Verbatim quote matching against JATS-derived text failed for 9 of 41 candidate quotes until whitespace was normalized: Elsevier/Cell Press text uses U+00A0 (no-break space) inside "1 g", "p = 0.004", "Figure 6A"; EBioMedicine uses U+202F (narrow no-break space) in "D + Q", "11 days"; Wiley uses U+2010 (hyphen) in "exercise‐mediated". Offsets in `_notes/fixture-candidates.md` are given on the raw tag-stripped text (script `xml2txt.py` in scratch) and the quotes are reproduced with ordinary spaces/hyphens, so the locator stage must apply an explicit normalization (NBSP/NNBSP → space, U+2010/U+2011 → "-") and record it.
