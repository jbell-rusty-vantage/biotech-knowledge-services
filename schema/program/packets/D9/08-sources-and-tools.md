## What broke, and what needs custom tooling

- **Unstable capture digests.** Firecrawl markdown changes per fetch for pages with widgets or session ids; offsets in such captures are capture-specific. Locators must carry exact + prefix + suffix (TEXT_QUOTE) and the verifier re-selects by context, as all 120 fixture locators did. Snapshot digests of projections carry `contentHashBasis NORMALIZED_TEXT`.
- **Archive substitution.** Wayback raw copies can be served from another capture date, with or without a redirect; the fixtures stage accepted a 2021-08-16 capture as 2021-12-09 and drew a wrong absence finding (Levels) from it. Every archive copy is now accepted only after its served memento-datetime and payload SHA1 match the CDX row (`archiveCheck` in locators.yaml); as-of findings are bounded by the nearest verified copies.
- **Audio.** No tool captured or transcribed audio; spoken spans known only from a publisher's media time (Lifespan 1:07:37) stay UNRESOLVED (SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE). Custom tooling needed: enclosure download + ASR with cue times + diarization, writing a text version with `textBasis ASR`.
- **Re-cut detection.** REPUBLISHES needs an audio fingerprint or transcript alignment; the RSS `?updated=` stamp is the only cheap signal that an enclosure changed.
- **Intent contract.** Claim occurrences, assessments and derived structure cannot be fully expressed in `biotech-knowledge-intent.v1` (tool-log #44); D9 needs an `assertion.claim` proposal (subject list, object, claimRef, containerRef, derivedFrom) or kernel-owned equivalents.
- **Not built:** bks-chunker, bks-projection-job, entity-resolution-agent (readiness 0–1); the fixtures used a scratch stand-in (`ft/D9/fx/gen.mjs`) whose rules are named in the chunks (`fixture-turn-window/v0`, `span-overlap/v1`, `entity-link/fixture-v0`).
- **Check stage spot-check (2026-10-08).** tool:podcast-rss (curl) re-fetched the *Lifespan* feed byte-identical (sha256:00a162e8…, 310,930 B; quote f1 at the same offsets with its 32-character context). tool:firecrawl-scrape (`--max-age 0`, scrape id 01a11d9a-3423-73f9-…) re-fetched the Huberman Lab ep 52 page as a third distinct digest (sha256:ad85a3a1…, 150,870 B): all 8 web-page locators of the hl52 fixture re-found with prefix + exact + suffix, and l1 back at its original offset 71,636 (the fixtures-stage copy had it at 71,491). Offsets in Firecrawl captures oscillate with page chrome; the TEXT_QUOTE context, not the offset, is the stable anchor.
