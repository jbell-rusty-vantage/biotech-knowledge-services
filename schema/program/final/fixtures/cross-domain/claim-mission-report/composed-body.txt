Evidence report: does NMN double blood NAD in about two weeks?

Claim under test
Claim under test: taking NMN daily for about two weeks doubles blood NAD levels on average in humans. [o1; l-o1]
David A. Sinclair stated it as a guest on Huberman Lab episode 52, published 2021-12-27, and gave clinical trials that were not yet published as its basis. [o1; l-o1-unpub]
BellLabs vetting sent the claim to this evidence mission with disposition RESEARCH. [as-vet-avg]

Interests of the claimant
The speaker's self-disclosure page, as archived on 2021-12-09, lists MetroBiotech, an EdenRoc company working on NAD boosters, from 2015 to present with the codes F, I, E, A, B (EdenRoc) and IP. [i-edenroc-f; i-edenroc-i; i-edenroc-e; i-edenroc-a; i-edenroc-b; i-metro-ip; w-metro; w-legend]
The captured episode page does not mention MetroBiotech or EdenRoc. [as-conflict]
The 14-day trial that best fits the stated basis was funded by Metro International Biotech, two of its authors are employees of that company, and a 2023 trial of the same formulation lists the speaker with a Metro International Biotech affiliation. [f-pencina-jg-funding; i-jcem-affiliation; as-res-metro; as-res-trial; pf-funding; pf-coi; pj-sinclair-aff]

Evidence for
In 32 adults given 1 000 mg of MIB-626, a crystalline beta-NMN formulation, once or twice daily or placebo for 14 days, MIB-626 was associated with substantial dose-related increases in blood NAD. [b-pencina-jg; pg-methods; pg-result]
NAD levels on Day 8 were similar to those on Day 14 in both dose groups. [b-pencina-jg; pf-day8]
Table 1 gives the Day-14 NAD Cmax above the pre-dose baseline as 23.0 ug/mL with 1 000 mg once daily, 40.4 ug/mL twice daily and 1.36 ug/mL with placebo. [m-pencina-cmax-1g; m-pencina-cmax-2g; m-pencina-cmax-pbo; pf-table-nad]

Evidence against and qualifications
The article reports increments above baseline, not fold changes, so the stated doubling cannot be read from the located text. [m-pencina-cmax-1g; as-synthesis; pf-baseline]
At 300 mg/day for 60 days, serum NAD+/NADH rose 38% from baseline against 14.3% with placebo, a difference that was not statistically significant. [b-huang; ph-38; ph-ns]
In a single-arm study of 300 mg/day for eight weeks, blood NAD decreased from 17.4 to 13.7 ng/mL. [b-morita; pm-design; pm-nad]
Trials at 250 to 900 mg/day report increases, but their first post-baseline blood samples were taken at 4 weeks or at day 30, the 60-day trial reports a within-group coefficient of variation of 29.2 to 113.3%, and the authors of the 12-week trial state that the increase may vary among individuals. [b-yi; m-kuerec-cv; b-okabe; po-visits; py-days; pk-cv; po-vary]
The 14-day and 28-day trials were funded by Metro International Biotech, the 60-day trial by the companies that made its NMN, and the 12-week trial by a company whose employees prepared the NMN; one 300 mg/day trial has an author employed by Effepharm, whose link to the product was not captured, and the single-arm study declares no conflict of interest. [as-synthesis; pf-funding; pj-grant; py-coi; py-product; po-funding; po-coi; ph-coi; pm-coi]

Verdict
Verdict PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED: the direction and the two-week time course rest on one trial funded by the speaker's affiliated company group, the average doubling is not stated in any located text, no located span contradicts the claim at its own dose and duration, and the only located evidence family independent of NMN makers is a 300 mg/day single-arm study that found a decrease. [as-synthesis; b-morita; pm-nad; pm-coi]

Open questions
An independent trial reporting blood NAD at about two weeks on about 1 g/day, or the absolute baseline values of the 14-day trial, would change this verdict. [as-synthesis]
The stronger statement that everyone taking 1 to 2 g/day doubles is a separate claim held for monitoring. [as-vet-univ]
