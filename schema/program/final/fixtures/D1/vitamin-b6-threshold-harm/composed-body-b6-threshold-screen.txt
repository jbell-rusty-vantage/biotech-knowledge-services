# Vitamin B6 threshold screen (BellLabs fixture report; research, not medical advice)

## Scope
Two US supplement labels are screened against two Australian (TGA) vitamin B6 thresholds for a person's own avoidance question (Q-D1-03). This is a cross-jurisdiction comparison at each label's maximum daily servings, never a regulatory finding.

## Threshold screen
c10a. Country Life P-5-P 50 mg (DSLD 326829): 34.2 mg/d (pyridoxine equivalent) vs AU 50 mg/d scheduling threshold (from 2027-06-01): not above.
c10b. Country Life P-5-P 50 mg (DSLD 326829): 34.2 mg/d (pyridoxine equivalent) vs AU 10 mg/d label-warning threshold (since 2022-03-01): above; an AU product at this dose would carry the warning statement.
c10c. EuroMedica Magnesium + P-5-P (DSLD 309443): 40 mg/d as declared (upper reading; 27.4 mg/d if the 10 mg is P5P mass) vs AU 50 mg/d scheduling threshold (from 2027-06-01): not above under either reading.
c10d. EuroMedica Magnesium + P-5-P (DSLD 309443): 40 mg/d as declared (27.4 mg/d if the 10 mg is P5P mass) vs AU 10 mg/d label-warning threshold (since 2022-03-01): above under either reading.
